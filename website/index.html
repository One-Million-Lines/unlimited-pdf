Privacy by design

Your files never leave your device.

UnlimitedPDF is a static Chrome extension experience: PDF processing happens locally in the browser, without a backend service and without tracking scripts. You can keep working after install even when you are offline.

Permissions are intentionally minimal: storage for your settings and activeTab for opening the current tab’s PDF when you choose to use the extension.

What local-first means

  • Files are processed in your browser, not uploaded to us.
  • No account, watermark, or daily task quota imposed by the product.
  • Works offline after install, subject to your device’s capacity.
  • Destructive or quality-changing actions include honest warnings.

FAQ

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. UnlimitedPDF is free and open source, with no account, watermark, or daily task quota imposed by us.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. PDF processing happens locally in your browser. UnlimitedPDF does not upload your files to a backend service.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the Chrome extension is installed, the included tools can work offline because processing runs on your device.

What are the limits?

There is no product-enforced usage quota from us, but very large or complex files can be limited by your device memory, browser limits, and available processing power.

Which permissions does it need?

UnlimitedPDF uses minimal Chrome permissions, including storage for settings and activeTab for opening the current tab’s PDF when requested.
